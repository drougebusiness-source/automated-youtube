Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private, internal tool operated by Rouge Ventures LLC. It uses YouTube API Services to upload original background-music videos to our own YouTube channel, Rest and Relaxation, on a daily schedule.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service YouTube Terms of Service Google Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Uploads videos to our own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API: videos.insert)
  • Sets each video's thumbnail (thumbnails.set)
  • Optionally adds videos to our own playlists (playlistItems.insert)

It is used only by Rouge Ventures LLC. It is not sold, licensed, or offered to anyone else, and it does not access any other YouTube channel or user's data.
